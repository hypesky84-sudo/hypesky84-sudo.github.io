Mailroom

A private email program used by Talbi Logistics LLC

Mailroom is a small email program that runs on the computers of Talbi Logistics LLC. It shows the company's own Gmail and Outlook mailboxes together in one window, so staff can read, reply to, send, organize and file business email in one place.

Mailroom is for internal use by Talbi Logistics only. It is not offered to the public, and it is only connected to email accounts owned by the company.

Mailroom signs in with Google and Microsoft only to work with those mailboxes. Email stays in Gmail and Outlook; Mailroom does not copy it to any server, sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.
