Mailroom Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Mailroom is a private email program used internally by Talbi Logistics LLC ("we"). This policy explains what Mailroom does with information from the Google and Microsoft email accounts connected to it.

Who uses Mailroom

Mailroom runs only on Talbi Logistics' own computers and is only connected to email accounts that belong to the company. It is not offered to the public.

What Mailroom accesses

When an account is connected, Mailroom uses Google's Gmail API (and Microsoft's Graph API for Outlook) to read, send, label, archive, move and delete email in that account, and to read the account's name and email address. It does this only so the person using Mailroom can manage that mailbox.

Where information is kept

Email stays in Gmail and Outlook. Mailroom shows it on screen and does not copy it to any server. The sign-in keys Mailroom needs are stored only on the company computer where Mailroom runs. Attachments are saved only when the user chooses to save them.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any information from connected accounts to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mailroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

An account can be disconnected at any time in Mailroom's settings, which deletes its sign-in keys from the computer. Access can also be removed from the Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
